FAQ

What families ask us most

When should we start?

For undergraduate applications, 12 to 18 months ahead. For graduate programs, 9 to 12 months ahead. More time means more room to strengthen a profile. If time is short, we focus first on what affects the outcome most.

Can we apply to several countries at once?

Yes, and it is common now, for example the US with Hong Kong, or the UK with Singapore. Deadlines and requirements differ, so we put every application on one timeline to avoid clashes.

Do you guarantee admission?

No, and no one should. Schools decide admissions and employers decide offers. Our job is to get the school list right and make every application as strong as it can be.

I already study or work abroad. Can you still help?

Yes. Career services can be booked on their own, without an admissions package. Single sessions such as a résumé review or a mock interview are also available.

How are fees set?

Admissions services are priced by stage and number of schools. Career coaching is priced as a full program or by session. You get a written quote and scope before signing. The first consultation is free.
